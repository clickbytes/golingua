These increments declare no morphology, valency, or construction coverage. Their draft lexical fit was assessed independently from Polish data; bindings remain pending and need owner review. Event nouns do not imply equivalent verb senses or argument frames. `Food` is commonly mass, with count use for kinds or servings. `Teaching` and `eating` are event nouns, not verb mappings. `Attribute` is selected as a quality noun; the alternate label `property` can also evoke ownership. Animal candidates distinguish classes and specified domestic species; fish-as-animal remains separate from prepared food, spider from insect, and cow from cattle generally. `Buyer` and `seller` name transaction roles without implying the associated verb senses; `singer` is a narrower vocal music role under `musician`. `Interpreter` is limited here to spoken or signed communication, while `translator` covers written content. Group nouns distinguish social relation, shared home, community identity, and recipient/gathering roles. Organization candidates distinguish a team, learner class, committee, commercial company, and member association. The existing school institution sense is now linked as a pending candidate, separately from school building and fish-group senses. Institution nouns retain the unresolved distinction between organizations and their physical sites or broader senses. `offspring` is a candidate for the age-independent child-of-person concept, separate from the existing young-person sense of `child`. The `older adult` candidate does not impose a specific age threshold. `Aunt` and `uncle` are gendered realizations of one kinship concept, so the English worklist records both senses without claiming a single gender-neutral equivalent. `Waiter` has gendered usage in some contexts; alternatives such as `server` need separate review. `Engineer` remains broad, with no specific discipline assumed. Spot checks against Cambridge Dictionary distinguish `acquaintance` from `friend`, the person sense of `stranger`, and the leisure travel sense of `tourist`; no definition text is copied. The ledger records that US `neighbor`/`traveler` correspond to UK `neighbour`/`traveller`, and excludes the separate UK sports-team sense of `tourist`. `SourceRef` identifies project-authored Go declarations with license `unknown`; no third-party definitions or forms were copied.

`UNREVIEWED` means editorial work has not yet been performed. It is not a deferral or an assertion that English lacks a direct form. The ledger is therefore a complete inventory scaffold but **not** completion of English editorial review or G07-04. Future increments should replace these rows only after decisions are made, with senses, lexical coverage, morphology/valency notes, provenance, reviewer, date, and rationale recorded explicitly.
